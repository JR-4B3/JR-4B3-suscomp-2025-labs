## Reflections

The adoption of Germany's electricity grid CO2e factor (0.32 kg CO2e/kWh) instead of Slovenia's significantly affects the use-phase emissions—generally increasing the annual use emissions estimates for devices. Lenovo and Apple device manufacture footprints remain based on official manufacturer data, while other device embodied footprints rely on open data sources. The comparison underscores that device manufacturing emissions dominate for short lifespans, but energy use emissions grow increasingly important with longer use and higher power draw. Extending the useful life of devices and increasing energy efficiency can substantially reduce overall carbon impact.
